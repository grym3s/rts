# sim/world — the playfield and its roster

Owns: `Bridges.cs` (T26: `BridgeStore.FromMap` = 4-connected `=` groups numbered by first cell in row-major order, each a `Bridge` with `BridgeHp` hit points and a Broken flag, then causeways from the scenario's `map.causeways` cell lists (Land when standing, so ships can't pass; unbuildable via `MarkNoBuild`; Water when down); hashed only once damaged, so untouched maps keep their golden hashes), `Terrain.cs` (T25: `Terrain` land/water/cliff/bridge, `MoveDomain` land/naval/hover/air, `TargetMask`, and the `Domains` rules: which domain stands on which tile, which weapon mask engages which domain, content parsing), `GameMap` (square walkable grid; T25 terrain layer, Land by default, cliffs also block; `IsWalkable(x, y)` is the land-unit check and `IsWalkable(x, y, domain)` the general one; `CanOccupy(..., onWater)` wants plain Land (never a bridge) or Water; `RingTouches` is the shore rule; `GameMap.FromJson` reads a scenario `map` — `terrain` rows of `.~#=` and/or `blocked` rects — for both tools/scenario and tools/sim-host; cell (cx,cy) spans [cx,cx+1]²; terrain `Block` plus a separate building-footprint layer, T31: `IsWalkable` = open terrain and no building, `IsTerrainWalkable`/`IsBlocked` = terrain only, `StructureEpoch` bumps on every place/release, not hashed since it follows from building units; T33 resource cells: `MarkResource` per vein, walkable but `CanOccupy` refuses them), `UnitStore` + `Unit` (positions, velocity, speed, radius; T25 `Domain` and `Targets` from the profile, not hashed — they follow from the catalog).
Reads: scenario data (via tools/scenario). Writes: its own structures only.
Runs at: created before the first tick; mutated by `sim/orders` + `sim/navigation` during a tick.
Tests: `../tests/NavigationTests.cs`, `../tests/FootprintTests.cs`, `../tests/TerrainDomainTests.cs`.
Do NOT: hold orders (that is `../orders`), pathfinding (`../navigation`), combat target/cooldown state (that is `../combat`).
Change impact: grid geometry or unit fields change scenario hashes — `map/effects/CONTEXT.md`.
