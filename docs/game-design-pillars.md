# Game design pillars (v0 — the vertical-slice fun test)

The slice "Skirmish 30" exists to answer one question: **is selecting units, telling them where to go, and watching them fight satisfying?**

Judged by both humans, played, not reasoned about:

1. Orders acknowledge within one frame (visible marker + unit reaction).
2. A group arrives without stringing out or clumping into a blob.
3. Units don't jitter, oscillate, or push through each other.
4. Targeting is legible — you can tell who is shooting whom and why.
5. Fights are decided by positioning and unit choice, not RNG.
6. A 30 v 30 engagement is readable at the default zoom.

If two of these still fail after tuning, the direction changes before anything else is built.

Explicitly not judged yet: economy, production, fog, factions, art, sound, campaign.
